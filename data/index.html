Data

A features-only dataset

Per-frame eye features and hand-made labels of spontaneous and deliberate eye closures, recorded with consumer webcams, so that anyone can test their own blink detectors. No images, no video, no faces.

Not released yet. The dataset will be published in data/public/ on GitHub once Study A has been labelled, together with a full datasheet (data/DATASHEET.md).

What is in it

Per-frame features (one row per video frame)

Per-frame feature columns
ColumnMeaning
t_msFrame capture time in milliseconds
faceWhether a face was found in the frame
earL, earREye aspect ratio of each eye, from six landmarks
blinkL, blinkRMediaPipe blink scores (blendshapes), 0 to 1
yaw, pitch, rollHead pose in degrees
n_facesNumber of faces in view
eye_ptsThe 12 eye landmarks (6 per eye) as normalised x, y; no other face points

Labels (one row per closure)

Made by hand by the experimenter in the labelling tool. Only closures confirmed by a person count as ground truth.

Label columns
ColumnMeaning
onset_ms, offset_msStart and end of the closure, on the frame clock
typespontaneous, deliberate-short, deliberate-long, wink-L or wink-R
labeller, labelled_atWho labelled it, and when

Session information

Cue times and types, block start and end times, the participant ID (P01, P02, …), age range, glasses (yes or no), lighting, camera model and the measured frame rate.

What is not in it

  • No images or video, and no face mesh beyond the 12 eye points.
  • No names, contact details or anything else that identifies a participant.
  • No audio.

Datasheet in brief

Purpose
Measure how well a consumer webcam separates deliberate from spontaneous blinks, and how much per-user calibration helps.
Who
One able-bodied adult, the author, in 6–8 detection sessions and 5 typing sessions. Data from any volunteers who take part are kept separately.
How
A laptop webcam at about 50–70 cm in normal, bright and dim light; about 12 minutes per session in the app’s study mode, with everything recorded on the laptop.
Labelling
Every closure labelled by hand by the author; two sessions re-labelled a week later to measure self-agreement.
Intended uses
Research on blink detection, calibration and blink-based text entry.
Not intended for
Identifying people, or any clinical or diagnostic use.

Privacy

  • The only participant is the author, who recorded and publishes the data.
  • Video is recorded on the laptop only to help with labelling, and is never published.
  • Files carry an ID only.
  • The public website and the Try it app record nothing: frames are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
