# Consent to take part: MorsEye blink study

## What this study is

MorsEye is a student research project by Saksham Sharma at BITS Pilani. It measures how well an ordinary laptop
webcam can tell deliberate eye closures apart from normal blinks, and how fast people can type Morse code with their
eyes. It is not a medical study, and nothing about your health is measured or inferred.

## What you will do

You will sit in front of a laptop for about 12 minutes. You will look at a dot, read some text, and close your eyes
or wink when cues appear on the screen. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can take a break at any time.

You may also be invited to a second, separate session of about 20 minutes in which you type short phrases with your
eyes. Taking part in the first session does not commit you to the second.

## What is recorded

- **Always:** numbers that describe your eyes in each video frame (how open each eye is, 12 points around the eyes,
  and the angle of your head), and the times when cues appeared. No picture of your face is stored.
- **Only if you tick the separate video box:** a video of your face, kept on this laptop only, so the researcher can
  mark exactly when each blink started and ended. It is never shared or published, and it is deleted as soon as
  labelling is finished.
- Your age range, whether you wear glasses, and the lighting and camera used. **Not** your name.

Everything is processed on this laptop. Nothing is sent over the internet during the session.

Your data is stored under a code (such as P07), never your name. Any list linking codes to names is kept on paper,
offline, and destroyed at the end of the project.

## How the data is used

The eye numbers (never the video) will be summarised in a research paper and published as an open dataset under a
licence that lets other researchers reuse them. Nobody can see your face from these numbers. Once the dataset is
public, copies that other people have already downloaded cannot be recalled.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary. There is no payment.
- You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and your data from that session will be deleted if you ask.
- You can ask for your data to be deleted later by quoting your code. Your files will be removed from the dataset
  and from any later release. Results already published in summary form cannot be changed.
- You must be 18 or older to take part.
