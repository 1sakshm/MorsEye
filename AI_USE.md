# AI assistance

MorsEye 2.0 was developed with assistance from AI. I used it as a coding, debugging, analysis, and writing assistant. I directed the project and reviewed its suggestions and outputs; I remain responsible for the final implementation, analysis, and paper.

## Areas where AI assisted

- **Application development:** Helped implement and debug the TypeScript/Vite application, including MediaPipe FaceLandmarker feature extraction, blink and closure detectors, hysteresis, calibration, duration segmentation, Morse decoding and commands, Study Mode (`/study`), the labelling tool (`/labeler`), and the `/try` interface.
- **Website and deployment:** Helped build and refine the website pages (`/`, `/how`, `/research`, `/data`, `/about`), visualisations, animations, deployment, and performance improvements.
- **Analysis and evaluation:** Helped write and debug Python analysis scripts, tests, data-processing pipelines, public Eyeblink8 analysis, case-study outputs, figures, and tables. The reported numerical results were generated by the analysis scripts from the available data.
- **Research planning:** Helped draft and refine the preregistration, including wording for research questions and hypotheses and proposed analysis details such as exclusion and stopping rules, the H2 non-inferiority margin, Holm correction families, and threshold-selection procedures. I reviewed and approved the preregistration before the first session.
- **Paper writing:** Helped draft and revise wording across the paper, including the abstract, introduction, related work, system description, study and results sections, discussion, limitations, ethics/data statements, and conclusion. I reviewed and edited the draft and am responsible for the final version. References and claims suggested by AI were checked against the cited sources.
- **Project documentation:** Helped draft or edit `PRD.md`, `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/public-data.md`, `docs/recruitment.md`, `docs/sessions.md`, `data/DATASHEET.md`, `data/public/README.md`, `README.md`, and `paper/README.md`.
- **Study materials and labelling support:** Helped prepare reading passages, study phrases, recruitment text, session checklists, and labelling-tool features. AI-assisted cues were treated as drafts only; I checked and labelled the response types myself.
- **Explainer video:** Helped write the narration script, the animation code and the soundtrack for the two-minute project video. The narration voice is synthetic (text-to-speech). Every number in the video is read from the analysis outputs, and the signal trace and eye landmarks come from session 1's log.
- **Public-data exploration:** Before the preregistration was written, the AI agent calculated annotated blink-length summaries for the eight Eyeblink8 videos and ran D1 and D3 on one video (video 9). This prior inspection is disclosed because it may affect interpretation of the subsequent analysis.

## My responsibilities

I set the project direction, reviewed and approved the preregistration, tested the application, checked the cited literature, collected the session data, and personally checked the response labels. I reviewed AI-assisted code and documents and am responsible for the decisions and claims in the final project. No automatic labels are treated as ground truth, and no reported result should be included unless it is supported by the analysis of actual data.

AI assistance does not replace the need to follow any applicable research, publication, or programme rules on AI use. Where relevant, I will check those rules before submitting or citing the work.
