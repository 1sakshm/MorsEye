Type by closing your eyes

MorsEye watches your eyelids through the webcam. A short deliberate closure is a dot, a longer one is a dash, and ordinary quick blinks are ignored. Everything runs on this device; no video leaves your browser.

  1. Allow the camera.
  2. Calibrate for about 20 seconds, so MorsEye learns what your open and closed eyes look like.
  3. Type. After setup you do not need a keyboard or mouse.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop, with light on your face, about an arm’s length from the screen.
